      Points depend on belts: upsets pay big. How it works · tap a name for their story.

      Belts are worth white 1 · blue 2 · purple 3 · brown 4 · black 5. The gap is your opponent's belt minus yours.

      • Win: 2 + your opponent's belt, plus the gap if they're a higher belt.
      • Loss: nothing lost against an equal or higher belt; lose the gap against a lower belt.
      • Draw: 1 + the gap (the lower belt gains, the higher belt drops).
