How it works

  1. 1
    Start a party. Open the player on the TV or a projector (one click there to allow sound).
  2. 2
    Guests scan the QR code with their phones, type their name and search the songbook, or find any karaoke video on YouTube and add it.
  3. 3
    The queue plays by itself. Fair-play mode rotates singers, the DJ panel can skip, pause, reorder and tidy up the shared songbook.
  4. 4
    Optional extras: a jukebox screen with a big QR code and song-code entry, a score screen after each song, and a printable songbook with song codes.

Songs play as YouTube embeds. A few channels don't allow their videos on other websites; those are hidden from the search, so just pick another version.
